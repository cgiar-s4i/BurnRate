📊 2027 BUDGET PLANNING NOTE: CALIBRATE ALLOCATIONS TO DEMONSTRATED DELIVERY
The 2026 budget is finalized and can’t be adjusted for current burn rates, but centers consistently underusing their allocation across 2025–2026 should be reviewed at the 2027 PORB, with allocations calibrated to demonstrated delivery. For overspending, the fix is procedural (advance authorization), not retrospective cuts — which induce precautionary underspending. IFPRI, ILRI, and CIAT, underperforming in both years, are the immediate review candidates. Any reduction needs a documented rationale discussed with the center and AoW lead.
⚠ STANDING RED FLAG: NON-CGIAR COLLABORATOR EXPENDITURE
Budget $2,246,790  |  Actual $61,265  |  Burn 2.7% — a standing concern every cycle. Spend moved off zero since April ($17K→$61K) but stays critically low. A second year of dormant external partners is a workplan design failure, not an execution delay.
⚠ ALLOCATION FLAG: AFRICARICE CHARGING AGAINST AN OVERSPENT AoW LINE
AfricaRice is posting spend to the wrong AoW. AoW 2 has a negative budget (−$189,279, already over-committed from carryforward) yet absorbed $223,800 — ~81% of the center’s $275,638 spend — taking available funds to −$413,079. Corrective action: stop charging the overspent AoW 2 and redirect postings to AoW 4, where the budget sits. Shola to confirm whether AoW 2 charges are miscoding (repost to AoW 4) or over-delivery needing advance authorization.
