📊 2027 BUDGET PLANNING NOTE: CALIBRATE ALLOCATIONS TO DEMONSTRATED DELIVERY
The 2026 budget is finalized and can’t be adjusted for current burn rates, but centers consistently underusing their allocation across 2025–2026 should be reviewed at the 2027 PORB, with allocations calibrated to demonstrated delivery. For overspending, the fix is procedural (advance authorization), not retrospective cuts — which induce precautionary underspending. CIAT and ILRI remain the immediate review candidates — both near 30% at mid-year after underperforming in 2025. IFPRI, flagged in prior months, has largely closed its gap (46.9%) and should be re-evaluated, subject to confirming the one-month catch-up postings are genuine. Any reduction needs a documented rationale discussed with the center and AoW lead.
✅ MID-YEAR MOVEMENT: STAFFING HAS CROSSED THE 50% LINE
Staffing, Chargebacks & Indirect reached 50.4% burn ($6.56M of $13.01M) — the first category on target, up from 40.8% in May. Personnel costs are now tracking to plan. The program’s remaining $3.44M gap is therefore concentrated almost entirely in Operations (18.8%) and Non-CGIAR collaborators (4.4%) — field activities and external partnerships, not overhead.
⚠ STANDING RED FLAG: NON-CGIAR COLLABORATOR EXPENDITURE
Budget $2,246,790  |  Actual $99,700  |  Burn 4.4% — a standing concern every cycle. Spend is inching up ($17K Apr → $61K May → $100K Jun) but is nowhere near pace at mid-year. Only five centers show any collaborator spend at all. AfricaRice ($202K budget, $0 spent) and CIMMYT remain the largest dormant blocks. A second year of unspent external partnerships is a workplan design failure, not an execution delay.
⚠ ALLOCATION FLAG: AFRICARICE STILL CHARGING AN OVERSPENT AoW LINE (DEEPENING)
The AoW miscoding flagged since May has worsened. AoW 2 holds a negative budget (−$199,689, over-committed from carryforward) yet now absorbs $260,219 — ~79% of the center’s $330,097 spend — pushing available funds to −$459,908. Meanwhile AoW 4, holding nearly all real headroom ($790,171 budget, $53,623 spent, 6.8%, $736,548 unspent), remains barely touched.
Corrective action: stop charging the overspent AoW 2 and redirect postings to AoW 4, where the budget sits. Shola to confirm whether the AoW 2 charges are miscoding (repost to AoW 4) or over-delivery needing advance authorization — and to act before the figure grows further.
