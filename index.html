📊 2027 BUDGET PLANNING NOTE: CALIBRATING ALLOCATIONS TO DEMONSTRATED DELIVERY CAPACITY
The 2026 program budget is finalized and cannot be adjusted in response to current-year burn rates. However, centers demonstrating a consistent pattern of underutilizing their allocation across 2025 and 2026 should be subject to formal review during the 2027 PORB process, with future allocations calibrated to demonstrated delivery capacity rather than to historical precedent alone. Budget resources should flow to where delivery is proven. For overspending, the appropriate response is procedural — requiring advance authorization for expenditure above budget — rather than retrospective budget reduction, which risks inducing precautionary underspending and is the worse outcome. IFPRI, ILRI, and CIAT are the most immediate candidates for 2027 allocation review, having underperformed in both 2025 and 2026. Any reduction in allocation should be accompanied by a documented rationale and discussed with the relevant center and AoW lead.

⚠ OUTCOME DELIVERY RISK: PORB COMMITMENTS vs. CURRENT SPEND

Budget underspend is a financial management concern. What the PORB crossref reveals is something more serious: outcome commitments that cannot be met because the delivery mechanisms have not been activated. For AoW 2, 3, and 4, many of the program’s most significant 2026 targets depend not on CGIAR execution alone but on contracted non-CGIAR partners — national research institutes, government agencies, and international organizations — delivering specific activities. When those partners are dormant after four months, the outcome gap is real and growing.
