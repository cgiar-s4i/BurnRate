📊 2027 BUDGET PLANNING NOTE: CALIBRATE ALLOCATIONS TO DEMONSTRATED DELIVERY
The 2026 budget is finalized and can’t be adjusted for current burn rates, but centers consistently underusing their allocation across 2025–2026 should be reviewed at the 2027 PORB, with allocations calibrated to demonstrated delivery. For overspending, the fix is procedural (advance authorization), not retrospective cuts — which induce precautionary underspending.
